# Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Last Updated: August 31, 2026**

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how Promptix handles payments, cancellations, refunds, project termination, and service-related disputes.

**Promptix** is an AI automation and technology service provider based in **Damak, Jhapa, Nepal**. Our services include AI automation, AI agents, workflow automation, website development, application development, graphic design, digital marketing, videography, video editing, consulting, training, and related technology services.

Because many of our services are **custom, project-based, and labor-intensive**, they are different from ordinary physical products or standardized digital products. Our refund policy therefore considers the work already performed, resources committed, project stage, third-party costs, and the specific agreement between Promptix and the client.

By making a payment for Promptix services, the client acknowledges and agrees to this Refund & Cancellation Policy, together with any applicable quotation, invoice, proposal, Service Agreement, or Statement of Work ("SOW").

Where a signed Service Agreement or SOW contains specific refund or cancellation terms, those terms will generally govern the relevant project.

---

## 1. General Refund Principle

Promptix aims to maintain a fair and transparent relationship with its clients.

We do **not** operate a blanket "no refund under any circumstances" policy.

However, because our services involve dedicated time, planning, technical resources, creative work, development, configuration, project scheduling, and sometimes third-party purchases, payments for work that has already been performed are generally non-refundable.

Where a project is cancelled before all contracted work has been completed, Promptix may calculate the amount earned for work already performed and refund any eligible unearned balance, subject to this Policy and the applicable project agreement.

# 2. Project Deposits and Advance Payments

Many Promptix projects require an advance payment or deposit before work begins.

The deposit may be used to:

- Reserve project time
- Allocate technical resources
- Conduct discovery and requirements analysis
- Design the solution
- Prepare project architecture
- Configure development environments
- Purchase or activate required services
- Begin development or automation work
- Schedule the project within our production workflow

### Before Work Begins

If a client cancels a project **before Promptix has commenced any project work**, Promptix may provide a refund of the eligible advance payment, after deducting:

- Non-refundable payment-processing charges
- Third-party costs already incurred
- Domain or hosting purchases
- Software or API charges
- Other documented costs specifically incurred for the client's project

### After Work Begins

Once project work has commenced, the advance payment generally becomes non-refundable to the extent that it has been earned through work performed or costs incurred.

"Work has commenced" may include:

- Requirements gathering
- Project planning
- Research
- Workflow design
- AI-agent design
- System architecture
- Wireframing
- Graphic design
- Programming
- Automation configuration
- API integration
- Testing
- Deployment preparation
- Content preparation
- Technical setup

# 4. Cancellation by the Client

A client may request cancellation of a project by contacting Promptix in writing.

The financial consequences of cancellation depend on the stage of the project.

### If Work Has Not Started

An eligible refund may be provided after deducting applicable non-refundable costs.

### If Work Is in Progress

Promptix may deduct the value of:

- Work already completed
- Development hours already used
- Design work already completed
- Automation configuration
- Research and planning
- Testing
- Project management
- Third-party costs
- Other committed project expenses

Any remaining eligible balance may be refunded.

### If the Project Is Completed

Payments for completed services are generally non-refundable.

# 5. Change of Mind

Refunds are generally not provided because a client:

- Changes their mind
- Changes their business strategy
- Decides not to use the completed system
- Finds another service provider
- Decides to discontinue the project for personal reasons
- Changes their business model after development has started
- No longer requires the service

This is because Promptix has already allocated time, technical resources, and expertise to the project.

# 6. Changes to Project Requirements

The project price is based on the agreed scope.

A request that changes or expands the original requirements may be considered a **Change Request**.

Examples include:

- Adding new website pages
- Adding new application features
- Adding new AI agents
- Adding additional integrations
- Changing the approved design substantially
- Connecting additional software
- Adding additional automation workflows
- Changing the project after development has begun

Additional charges may apply to Change Requests.

Promptix will generally communicate the additional cost and expected timeline before carrying out substantial additional work.

A client cannot request a refund simply because they later request features that were not included in the original scope.

# 8. Website Development Refunds

Website development is a custom service.

Refunds are generally not available for completed development work, design work, coding, testing, or deployment.

If a client cancels before completion, Promptix may calculate the amount corresponding to the work already performed and deduct that amount from any eligible refund.

If the website does not meet the agreed specifications, Promptix will first have a reasonable opportunity to correct the issue within the agreed scope.

A technical issue or bug that results from Promptix's implementation will normally be addressed through the applicable support or bug-fix period rather than through an automatic refund.

# 18. Refunds Due to Promptix's Failure to Deliver

Promptix believes that a refund policy should also protect clients when the service provider is responsible for a failure.

If Promptix is unable to deliver a contracted service because of a material failure on our part, the client should first notify us and provide a reasonable opportunity to correct or complete the service.

Depending on the circumstances, Promptix may provide:

- Corrective work at no additional cost
- Additional development time
- Replacement service
- Project credit
- Partial refund for the undelivered portion
- Full refund where Promptix has received payment but is unable to provide the contracted service

Refunds will generally relate to the portion of the service that Promptix has not earned.

# 19. Technical Bugs and Warranty

A bug is different from a new feature.

If Promptix delivers a website, application, or automation that does not function according to the agreed specifications due to an error in our implementation, Promptix will make reasonable efforts to correct the issue during the applicable support or bug-fix period.

A bug-fix request does not normally create an automatic right to a refund.

The applicable project agreement may specify the exact duration of the bug-fix or warranty period.

# 20. No Refund for Unused Services

Unless expressly agreed otherwise, unused portions of a purchased custom service are not automatically refundable.

For example, a client cannot automatically request a refund simply because:

- They did not use the completed website
- They stopped using an automation
- They did not use their remaining support time
- Their business closed
- Their business changed direction
- They did not launch the completed application

# 21. Exceptional Refunds

Promptix may, at its discretion, provide a partial or full refund in exceptional circumstances even when a refund is not otherwise required by this Policy.

Examples may include:

Providing an exceptional refund in one situation does not create an automatic entitlement to a refund in future situations.
